02 — How it works

Look up.
Know where you are.

  1. Observe

    The sky is the reference

    Stars at night, the sun by day. Nothing is transmitted, so there is nothing to detect and nothing to jam.

  2. Resolve

    Absolute, not relative

    Each fix is computed from the sky itself, so error does not accumulate. VIO and map-matching run alongside for precise short-term positioning.

  3. Output

    Straight to the autopilot

    MAVLink into ArduPilot or PX4. The module plugs in as a payload — no ground infrastructure required.
