02 — Operating modes

Night and day.

Both modes are passive and zero-RF, and neither accumulates error.

Night

StarNav

Absolute heading from the stars. No error accumulation.

Day

SolarNav

Absolute heading from the sun, corrected continuously through daylight hours.

Fallback

VIO + map-matching

Precise short-term positioning while the sky is unavailable.

Output

MAVLink

Straight into ArduPilot or PX4.
