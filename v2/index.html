02 — The system

One module. Two skies.

StarNav and SolarNav are two modes of the same module: the night sky and the sun give an absolute heading that does not drift.

Full specification→

StarNav

04 — Why it holds

Survives jamming. Does not drift.

Can't be jammed

It depends on no external signal, so there is nothing to block or spoof.

No error accumulation

Every fix is absolute, so the error does not grow with time like inertial drift.

Nothing to detect

Zero RF emissions: the module only receives light from the sky.
